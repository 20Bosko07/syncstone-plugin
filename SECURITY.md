# Security policy

Syncstone encrypts your notes on your device. Security problems matter to us, so thank you for telling us.

## Report a problem privately

Please **do not open a public issue** for a security problem.

1. Use **Report a vulnerability** on the [Security tab](https://github.com/syncst-one/syncstone-plugin/security) of this repository, or
2. write to **support@syncst.one** with the subject "Security".

Tell us what you found, how to reproduce it and what you think it could cause. Please do not test against other people's accounts and do not access data that is not yours.

We aim to answer within 7 days and to tell you when it is fixed. We are happy to credit you if you want.

## What counts

- Weaknesses in how the plugin encrypts, stores or sends data
- Ways to read or change someone else's notes
- Leaks of session tokens, passphrases or keys
- Problems in the sync protocol that lose or expose data

Problems in the Syncstone service itself (website, accounts, payments) are welcome at the same address.

## Supported versions

Only the latest release gets fixes. Update through Obsidian or BRAT.

# Code of conduct

Syncstone is a small project and we want it to stay a friendly place to ask, report and help.

- Be kind and patient. People come with different experience.
- Talk about the problem, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or spam.
- Do not post other people's private data, and do not post your own notes or passphrase.
- Assume good intent, and say clearly when something is unclear to you.

We may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and we may block people who keep breaking them.

If someone treats you badly, write to **support@syncst.one**. We read it and keep what you tell us private.
